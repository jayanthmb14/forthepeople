## 1. Infrastructure projects (InfraProject)

Every district's list was checked row by row: all well-known projects,
the biggest ~15–20 by budget, every row that is not really a project,
and near-duplicate names. The web-search quota for the session (200
searches, shared by five checkers) ran out part-way, so later rows were
checked only through official pages that could be opened directly
(PIB, PM India, MMRDA, HMRL, K-RIDE, MTP …) and named news reports.
Anything that could not be confirmed is in the "unverified" list below
and is **not** changed.

Rules used: status words are the tracker's own (`PROPOSED`, `APPROVED`,
`TENDER_ISSUED`, `UNDER_CONSTRUCTION`, `PARTIALLY_OPERATIONAL`, `DELAYED`,
`STALLED`, `CANCELLED`, `COMPLETED`); the completion date is the day it
opened or was inaugurated; budgets are whole rupees (shown here in crore
for reading). Every updated or confirmed row gets `lastVerifiedAt =
2026-09-27`. Deleting a project also deletes its timeline rows
(`InfraUpdate`, onDelete: Cascade).

**No census was held in 2021.** It was postponed because of COVID-19 and
became Census 2027: house-listing ran April–September 2026 and the
population count's reference date is 00:00 on **1 March 2027** (1 Oct 2026
for Ladakh and snow-bound areas) —
[PIB, "Census 2027: India's First Digital Enumeration Exercise", 25 Apr 2026](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressNoteDetails.aspx?NoteId=158344&ModuleId=3).
The Registrar General's official population projections (Technical Group,
2020) are state-level only, so there is no official district figure after
2011. The overview's "Population" tile picks the newest row whose source
starts with "Census of India", so the Mandya and Bengaluru 2021 rows were
being shown to citizens as census counts.
